How to make: Shrimp & Grits

Shrimp and Grits is a comforting Southern classic made with creamy cheese grits and succulent shrimp in a rich, flavorful sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 4 cups water
  • salt and pepper
  • 1 cup stone-ground grits
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1 pound shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 6 slices bacon, chopped
  • 4 teaspoons lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • 1 cup thinly sliced scallions
  • 1 large clove garlic, minced

Instructions:

Grits:

  1. Bring water to a boil.
  2. Add salt and pepper.
  3. Add grits and cook until water is absorbed, about 20 to 25 minutes.
  4. Make sure to whisk them to get the lumps out and ensure a smooth, creamy consistency. Remove from heat and stir in butter and cheese.

Shrimps:

  1. Fry the bacon in a large skillet until browned; drain well.
  2. In the same grease, add shrimp.
  3. Cook until shrimp turn pink, this ensures that the shrimp will stay tender as they continue to cook in the sauce.
  4. Add lemon juice, chopped bacon, parsley, scallions and garlic. Saute for 3 minutes.
  5. Serve the shrimp over the grits.
  6. After spooning the grits into a serving bowl, add the shrimp mixture on top and serve.
  7. You can also mix the shrimp into the grits. Serve immediately.
